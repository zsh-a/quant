## Hierarchy of concepts

1. **Bar types** — single-bar shape (trend, doji, signal, climactic,
   reversal, ii, iii, outside, shaved).
2. **Patterns** — multi-bar setups built from bar shapes plus structural
   context (Hn/Ln, two-bar reversal, wedge, double top/bottom, final flag,
   micro-channel, breakout, breakout-pullback, failed breakout, MTR,
   measured-move target).
3. **Regimes** — seven structural states the market can be in
   (strong/weak bull/bear, tight/broad TR, breakout, climax→reversal).
4. **Trader's equation** — the only acceptance criterion for a setup.
5. **Always-in** — the binary "which side would you be on right now?"
   answer that gates every entry.

## Vocabulary conventions

- `H1..H4` = bull pullbacks (1st..4th in the same up-leg).
- `L1..L4` = bear pullbacks.
- `BP` = breakout-pullback. `FBO` = failed breakout. `MTR` = major trend reversal.
- `MM` = measured move (default profit target).
- `R` = unit risk (entry − stop). Reward is expressed as multiples of R.
- "1 tick" beyond the signal-bar extreme is the canonical entry/stop offset.

## Process the LLM should follow each bar

1. Classify the latest bar (`bar_types`).
2. Update `always_in` per `always_in_rules`.
3. Identify the active `regime`.
4. Search for `patterns` whose `detection.requires` are satisfied.
5. For each candidate, compute the **trader's equation** with a probability
   chosen from the bucket consistent with the regime, then accept only if
   expectancy ≥ 1R after costs.
6. Emit the chosen action with a reference to the matched `pattern.name`
   and the satisfied `detection` predicates.

## Bar types

### `trend_bull`

*aliases:* `strong_bull`, `bull_trend_bar`

Bull bar with body_pct > 50 and close in the upper third of the range. Indicates one-sided buying pressure during the bar.

**detection**

```yaml
body_pct_min: 50
close_position: high
is_bull: true
is_trend: true
```

**positive examples**

- body=78 close=high (e.g. o=100 h=110 l=99 c=109)
- body=62 close=high follow-through bar after H1 trigger

**negative examples**

- body=78 close=mid — strong body but upper wick rejects → not trend_bull
- body=20 close=high — bull but doji-ish → doji_bull

### `trend_bear`

*aliases:* `strong_bear`, `bear_trend_bar`

Bear bar with body_pct > 50 and close in the lower third of the range. One-sided selling pressure during the bar.

**detection**

```yaml
body_pct_min: 50
close_position: low
is_bull: false
is_trend: true
```

**positive examples**

- body=70 close=low follow-through after L1 trigger

**negative examples**

- body=70 close=mid — long lower wick rejects → not trend_bear

### `doji_bull`

*aliases:* `weak_bull`, `bull_body_small`

Bull-coloured bar with body_pct <= 10 — effectively two-sided.

**detection**

```yaml
is_bull: true
is_doji: true
```

**positive examples**

- body=4 close=mid — barely-bull doji inside a tight range

**negative examples**

- body=30 — small but not doji

### `doji_bear`

*aliases:* `weak_bear`, `bear_body_small`

Bear-coloured bar with body_pct <= 10.

**detection**

```yaml
is_bull: false
is_doji: true
```

### `signal_bar`

*aliases:* `setup_bar`

A bar whose extreme defines the next bar's stop-entry price. In Brooks practice a *good* signal bar is a trend bar in the direction of the intended trade. Detection here is the structural role; quality is graded separately by the L3 detectors.

**detection**

```yaml
role: setup_for_next_bar_stop_entry
```

### `climactic_bar`

*aliases:* `climax`, `blowoff`

Trend bar that is unusually large (range >> ATR) and closes near the extreme in the direction of the dominant trend. Often marks exhaustion.

**detection**

```yaml
close_position_in_trend: extreme
ema_atr_distance_abs_min: 2.0
is_trend: true
```

**positive examples**

- after 6 consecutive bull bars: trend_bull with range = 2.4*ATR

**negative examples**

- trend bar 1.2*ATR — large but not blow-off

### `reversal_bar`

*aliases:* `pin_bar`, `hammer_or_shooting_star`

Bar with a wick >= 40% of its range pointing against the prior swing, and a close that rejects that direction (close in opposite third).

**detection**

```yaml
is_reversal_bar: true
```

**positive examples**

- long lower wick, close in upper third after a 5-bar bear leg

**negative examples**

- wick=20% — too small to qualify

### `ii`

*aliases:* `inside_inside`

Two consecutive inside bars (the second inside the first).

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- feat[-1].is_inside_bar == true
- feat[0].is_inside_bar == true
```

**positive examples**

- bar(t-1) inside bar(t-2); bar(t) inside bar(t-1)

### `iii`

*aliases:* `inside_inside_inside`

Three consecutive inside bars; a tight squeeze, often a breakout setup.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- feat[-2].is_inside_bar == true
- feat[-1].is_inside_bar == true
- feat[0].is_inside_bar == true
```

### `outside_bar`

*aliases:* `engulfing`

Bar whose high > prior high AND low < prior low.

**detection**

```yaml
is_outside_bar: true
```

### `shaved_bar`

*aliases:* `shaved_top`, `shaved_bottom`

Trend bar whose close equals the extreme in the trend direction (no wick on the trend side). Strong continuation signal.

**detection**

```yaml
is_trend: true
no_wick_on_trend_side: true
```

## Patterns

### `h1` — High 1 (first pullback in bull leg)

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

In a leg up (always_in == long, current_leg_dir == up), price pulls back
with one or more bear bars and forms a bull reversal bar whose high is
the trigger. H1 is the *first* such pullback after always-in flipped
long (or after the previous Hn structure reset).

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- structure.always_in == long
- structure.current_leg_dir == up
- pullback_bear_bars_count >= 1
- pullback_bear_bars_count <= 2
- signal_bar.kind == bull_reversal_or_bull_trend
signal_bar: bull bar at or near the pullback low (ideally close in upper third)
```

**entry** — signal_bar.high + 1 tick (stop-buy on next bar)

**stop** — signal_bar.low - 1 tick

**target** — measured_move from leg origin OR last swing high

**common_misreads**

- 在 broad trading range 中将顶部反弹的小阳线当 H1 — 没有上涨腿背景，应忽略
- 把 always_in == short 时的小反弹叫 H1（应叫 L1 of bear）

### `h2` — High 2 (second pullback in bull leg)

*typical_probability:* `0.60`

**setup**

A second discrete pullback in the same up-leg: after H1 either fails
or is consumed, price pulls back again, prints another bull reversal
whose high triggers entry. Higher quality than H1 in tight bull channels.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- structure.always_in == long
- leg_count_down >= 2
- signal_bar at or below leg-1 pullback low or marginally lower
signal_bar: bull reversal at or below the leg-1 low; trend body preferred
```

**entry** — signal_bar.high + 1 tick (stop-buy)

**stop** — signal_bar.low - 1 tick

**target** — measured_move OR last swing high

**common_misreads**

- 在 broad TR 中错把 L2 反弹当 H2 — TR 没有 always-in 多头方向
- H1 触发后的延续小阳线被算成新的 H2 — 必须是新的 leg down 后的反弹

### `h3` — High 3 (third pullback — wedge bull flag)

*typical_probability:* `0.50`

**setup**

Three discrete bull pullbacks in the same leg, often forming a wedge bull
flag (each pullback shallower or each high higher). Lower-probability
because the trend is older.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- structure.always_in == long
- leg_count_down >= 3
- signal_bar.kind == bull_reversal_or_bull_trend
```

**entry** — signal_bar.high + 1 tick

**stop** — signal_bar.low - 1 tick

**target** — measured_move; trail aggressively

**common_misreads**

- 未确认是 wedge 而是 3-push down → 实际是 reversal 而非 continuation

### `h4` — High 4 (fourth pullback — late, often reverses)

*typical_probability:* `0.40`

**setup**

Four discrete bull pullbacks in the same leg. Brooks treats H4 as a
*reversal* setup more often than continuation; many H4s mark the start
of the bear leg.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- structure.always_in == long
- leg_count_down >= 4
```

**entry** — signal_bar.high + 1 tick (continuation) OR L1 short on failure

**stop** — signal_bar.low - 1 tick

**target** — scalp; tight risk/reward

**common_misreads**

- 把 H4 当 H1 入场（极易亏）

**entry** — signal_bar.low - 1 tick (stop-sell)

**stop** — signal_bar.high + 1 tick

**entry** — signal_bar.low - 1 tick

### `ii_breakout` — ii (inside-inside) breakout

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

After two consecutive inside bars, the next bar breaks one extreme. The
ii body is the signal bar; entry is on stop in the breakout direction.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- bar(t-1).is_inside_bar == true
- bar(t).is_inside_bar == true
signal_bar: the second inside bar
```

**entry** — ii_high + 1 tick (long) OR ii_low - 1 tick (short)

**stop** — opposite extreme of the ii

**target** — measured_move = ii.range projected from breakout level

**common_misreads**

- 在 strong trend 中只交易 with-trend 突破；逆势 ii 突破极易失败

*typical_probability:* `0.60`

**setup**

Stronger compression than ii; breakout has larger expected range.

**detection**

```yaml
- bar(t-2).is_inside_bar == true
- bar(t-1).is_inside_bar == true
- bar(t).is_inside_bar == true
```

**entry** — iii_high + 1 tick (long) OR iii_low - 1 tick (short)

### `two_bar_reversal` — Two-bar reversal

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

A trend bar in one direction immediately followed by a trend bar in the
opposite direction with a similar-sized body, both at a swing extreme.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- bar(t-1).is_trend == true
- bar(t).is_trend == true
- bar(t-1).is_bull != bar(t).is_bull
- at_or_near_swing_extreme(bar(t-1))
```

**entry** — second bar's extreme in the new direction +/- 1 tick

**stop** — opposite extreme of the two-bar combo

**target** — measured_move OR opposite swing

### `wedge` — Wedge (3-push)

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

Three pushes (pullbacks/highs or pullbacks/lows) where momentum decays:
slope of swing-extreme regression flattens. Often a reversal setup.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- swing_count_in_direction >= 3
- abs(slope_of_swings) decreasing across pushes
```

**entry** — 1 tick beyond the reversal-bar after push 3

**stop** — 1 tick beyond push-3 extreme

**target** — start of wedge OR measured_move from wedge height

**common_misreads**

- 未到 push 3 就当 wedge 反转 — 等第三推确认

### `double_top` — Double Top

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

Two swing highs within ~1 ATR of each other separated by a pullback;
neckline is the intervening swing low. Bear reversal or pullback.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- two_recent_swing_highs_within_1_atr
- intervening_swing_low_present
```

**entry** — neckline - 1 tick (stop-sell) OR bar after second-high reversal bar

**stop** — 1 tick above the second high

**target** — measured_move = (high - neckline) projected below neckline

**common_misreads**

- 在 strong bull trend 中把任意双高当反转 — 需要弱化的二推或 climax

### `double_bottom` — Double Bottom

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

Mirror of double_top — bull reversal.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- two_recent_swing_lows_within_1_atr
- intervening_swing_high_present
```

**entry** — neckline + 1 tick (stop-buy)

**stop** — 1 tick below the second low

**target** — measured_move = (neckline - low) projected above neckline

### `final_flag` — Final Flag

*typical_probability:* `0.50`

**setup**

A tight pullback late in a trend that breaks out *briefly* in the trend
direction and immediately fails — the breakout bar is reversed within
1-2 bars. Flag is the last with-trend pause before reversal.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- leg_age >= 10 bars
- tight_pullback_after_climax == true
- breakout_then_reverse_within_2_bars == true
```

**entry** — 1 tick beyond the failure bar in the new (counter-trend) direction

**stop** — 1 tick beyond the failed-breakout extreme

**target** — start of final flag OR last major swing

**common_misreads**

- early-trend pullback 当 final flag — 至少要 climax 或长腿背景

### `micro_channel` — Micro Channel

*typical_probability:* `0.65`

**setup**

A tight one-sided sequence of bars where each bar's low (in a bull
micro-channel) is above the prior bar's low. Often produces no
pullback for many bars; first pullback is high-probability.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- structure.micro_channel_top != null OR structure.micro_channel_bot != null
- consecutive_with_trend_bars >= 4
- no_overlap_with_prior_bar_for_4+_bars
```

**entry** — first pullback's signal_bar in trend direction +/- 1 tick

**stop** — 1 tick beyond signal_bar

**target** — measured_move from channel height; trail aggressively

**common_misreads**

- 在 micro_channel 内逆势 fade — 直到通道破裂前几乎必亏

### `breakout` — Range/structure breakout

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

**setup**

Close beyond the prior N-bar (default 20) extreme. With-trend bar

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- feat.is_breakout_up_20 == true OR feat.is_breakout_down_20 == true
- feat.is_trend == true
```

**entry** — breakout bar close +/- a tick (or stop-entry beyond breakout high/low)

**stop** — 1 tick beyond opposite extreme of breakout bar

**target** — measured_move from range height

**common_misreads**

- doji/小体突破当强突破 — 要求 trend body + 极端 close

### `breakout_pullback` — Breakout Pullback (BP)

*typical_probability:* `0.65`

**setup**

First pullback after a confirmed breakout; entry on with-trend signal
bar that doesn't break back inside the prior range.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- structure.breakout_state in (bull_breakout, bear_breakout) within last 5 bars
- pullback_does_not_close_back_inside_range

```

**entry** — signal_bar extreme in trend direction +/- 1 tick

**stop** — 1 tick beyond signal_bar

**target** — measured_move from breakout

### `failed_breakout` — Failed Breakout (FBO)

*typical_probability:* `0.60`

**setup**

A breakout bar that closes back inside the prior range within 1-5

**detection**

```yaml

- feat.is_breakout_up_20 == true OR feat.is_breakout_down_20 == true
- close_back_inside_prior_range_within_5_bars == true
```

**stop** — 1 tick beyond the failed-breakout extreme

### `mtr` — Major Trend Reversal (MTR)

*typical_probability:* `0.65`

**setup**

Lower-high after a higher-low (or higher-low after lower-high) with
a wedge/climax preceding the reversal. Brooks' classic reversal pattern;
higher confidence than a single double-top.

**detection**

```yaml
requires:
- wedge_or_climax_preceded == true

```

**entry** — 1 tick beyond the second-leg signal bar

**stop** — 1 tick beyond the lower-high (bull→bear) or higher-low (bear→bull)

### `measured_move` — Measured Move (MM)

*typical_probability:* `0.55`

## Trader's equation (canonical block)

**formula**

```
expectancy = P(win) * reward - P(loss) * risk
```

For every setup the trader must estimate (1) probability of reaching
target before stop, (2) reward (target - entry) and (3) risk (entry - stop).
A setup is acceptable when expectancy >= 1R after costs.

**probability_buckets**

- `0.40` — speculative scalp — only valid if R:R >= 2:1
- `0.55` — default for solid breakouts and Hn/Ln in normal trends
- `0.65` — strong setups: micro-channel first pullback, BP, MTR
- `0.75` — high-conviction confluence: H2 in strong bull + 20EMA bounce
- `0.85` — rare 'A+' setups (multiple confluence + favourable HTF)

**worked_examples**

```yaml
- expectancy_R: 0.69
  name: h2_in_strong_bull
  probability: 0.65
  reward_ticks: 16.0
  risk_ticks: 8.0
  verdict: take
- expectancy_R: -0.4
  name: counter_trend_l2_in_strong_bull
  probability: 0.3
  reward_ticks: 8.0
  risk_ticks: 8.0
  verdict: skip — negative expectancy
```

# Trader's Equation — applied notes

The taxonomy section `trader_equation` defines the formula and the canonical
probability buckets. This section adds the *reasoning rules* the LLM must
apply when picking a bucket for a live setup.

## Formula recap

```
expectancy = P(win) * reward − P(loss) * risk
P(loss) = 1 − P(win)
```

Working in units of `R` (R = risk = entry − stop in absolute price), reward
is expressed as `reward_R = reward / R`. The acceptance rule used everywhere
in this codebase is:

```
accept iff (P(win) * reward_R − (1 − P(win))) >= 1.0
```

The "≥ 1R" threshold accounts for slippage, commission, and the empirical
fact that probability estimates are biased optimistically.

## Choosing a probability bucket

Pick the **lowest** bucket consistent with the regime/pattern combination.
When in doubt, drop one bucket — over-estimating P(win) is the single
biggest source of negative expectancy.

| Bucket | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| 0.40 | Counter-trend in a strong trend; speculative scalps; H4/L4 continuation |
| 0.55 | Default for Hn/Ln in a normal trend, breakout from a trading range |
| 0.65 | Strong setups: micro-channel first pullback, BP, MTR, double top/bottom at HTF level |
| 0.75 | High-conviction confluence: H2 in strong bull + 20EMA bounce + measured-move target intact |
| 0.85 | Rare 'A+' setups — only when **multiple** independent edges align: HTF level + LTF MTR + climactic prior bar + reversal-bar quality |

## Adjustments

- **Regime mismatch** (e.g. taking an L2 short in a strong bull trend):
  drop **two** buckets, not one. Brooks calls these "always-wrong" trades.
- **Late in the trend** (leg_age > 20 bars or 4th+ pullback): drop one bucket.
- **Climactic bar within last 3 bars**: drop one bucket for continuation,
  raise one bucket for fade-the-climax setups.
- **Tight stop available** (`R < 0.5 ATR`): probability unaffected, but the
  reward needed for ≥1R expectancy is halved — these are the most attractive
  entries.

trades. Use them as anchors when grading an unfamiliar setup:

- `h2_in_strong_bull`: P=0.65, R=8t, reward=16t → expectancy ≈ 0.69R
  (close to threshold; take only with confluence).
- `counter_trend_l2_in_strong_bull`: P=0.30, R=8t, reward=8t → expectancy
  ≈ −0.40R (skip).

When in doubt, write the equation out explicitly in the trade-log comment
and let the LLM/operator audit the probability assignment.

## Always-in rules

`always_in` is the side a discretionary trader would choose to be on if
forced to take a position right now. It flips on a *close* beyond the
prior N-bar (default 20) extreme that is also confirmed by structure.

**flip_to_long_when**

- close > max(high[-N..-1]) AND bar(t).is_trend == true AND bar(t).is_bull == true
- OR after a successful breakout-pullback long

**flip_to_short_when**

- close < min(low[-N..-1]) AND bar(t).is_trend == true AND bar(t).is_bull == false
- OR after a successful breakout-pullback short

**stay_neutral_when**

- ema_relation == at AND no recent breakout AND range_height_atr < 2.0

**hysteresis** — once set, do not flip on chop; require fresh breakout or MTR

# Common Misreads — checklist before entering

The taxonomy ships a `common_misreads` block per pattern. This file
collects the cross-cutting failure modes the LLM must screen for before
emitting a signal.

## 1. Confusing a trading range with a trend

A leg inside a broad TR looks like a trend in isolation. The fix:

- Compute `range_height_atr = (range_high − range_low) / atr14`. If the
  current swing is fully inside a 2+ ATR range with `always_in == neutral`,
  the regime is `broad_trading_range`, **not** a trend.
- In a TR, every Hn/Ln upgrade should be downgraded one bucket and the
  default action is to **fade the extremes**, not chase pullbacks.

## 2. Mis-counting Hn / Ln

`Hn` and `Ln` are counted from the start of the **current** leg, reset by:

- a flip of `always_in`,
- a confirmed swing in the opposite direction beyond the prior leg's extreme,
- or a clear MTR.

If the prior swing is unconfirmed, **wait**. Counting H3 when it's actually
a fresh H1 in a new leg leads to "late entry" expectancy errors.

## 3. Treating doji bars as signal bars

Brooks signal bars must be **trend bars** in the entry direction (`is_trend
== true`, `close_position` matches direction). A doji signal bar:

- doubles the probability of being run-stop within the next 2 bars,
- should drop the bucket by one,
- and is invalid in tight ranges (the next bar is as likely to be doji too).

## 4. Counter-trend H4/L4 entries treated as continuation

H4/L4 are **reversal-biased** in Brooks practice. If your detector says
"H4 long in a strong bull", the more likely interpretation is "L1 short
forming". Default to skipping continuation, and consider the opposite-side
setup once a reversal bar prints.

## 5. Misreading micro-channels

Inside a micro-channel:

- **Do not fade.** Counter-trend trades inside a fresh micro-channel are
  the highest-loss action category in Brooks' literature.
- The first pullback after the micro-channel breaks is the highest-quality
  entry; wait for it.

## 6. Ignoring `always_in` hysteresis

`always_in` does **not** flip on every breakout bar. The hysteresis rule
in `taxonomy.yaml -> always_in_rules` requires a confirmed close beyond
the N-bar extreme **plus** a trend bar in the breakout direction. LLM
output that toggles `always_in` every other bar is wrong.

## 7. Climactic bar interpretation

A climactic bar is **not** itself a continuation entry — it is exhaustion.
Continuation after a climax requires either:

- a `breakout_pullback` whose pullback does not close back inside the
  pre-climax range, or
- a `final_flag` setup (which then *reverses*, not continues).

## 8. Measured-move target abuse

`measured_move` is a **target-side** concept; it never appears as an
`entry`. If a candidate signal lists MM in the entry field, the LLM has
hallucinated. Use MM only to size the take-profit.

## 9. Ignoring regime when grading

The same Hn pattern has very different probabilities in different regimes:

- H2 in `strong_bull_trend`: bucket 0.65–0.75
- H2 in `tight_trading_range`: bucket 0.45 (effectively a fade-buy near low)
- H2 in `weak_bear_trend`: skip — it's an L1 short instead

Always emit the matched regime alongside the pattern so the grader can
audit the bucket choice.

## 10. Stop placement off by one tick

Stops are 1 tick **beyond** the signal-bar extreme, not at it. Off-by-one
stops are run intra-bar by spread/normal noise. Use:

- long entry: `signal_bar.high + 1 tick`, stop `signal_bar.low − 1 tick`.
- short entry: `signal_bar.low − 1 tick`, stop `signal_bar.high + 1 tick`.
